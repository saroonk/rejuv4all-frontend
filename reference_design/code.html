DAILY PROTOCOL

Make It a Ritual. Make It Yours.

Sensory ease meets architectural care. Follow our four-step ritual for maximum biological absorption.

The Four Stages of Sensorial Grooming
01

PREPARE — Warm & Breathe

Dispense 2–4 golden drops into warm palms. Rub hands together to activate active botanical lipid chains, inhaling the grounding woody cedar and warm amber notes.

02

APPLY — Mid-Lengths to Ends

Gently glide fingers like a wide-tooth comb through damp or towel-dried strands. Focus primarily on delicate mid-lengths and dry ends where cuticle stress is highest.

03

MASSAGE — Follicle Circulation

Gently press residual elixir upward into parting lines and temples. Use slow, circular fingertips to stimulate circulation and promote balanced, hydrated scalp skin.

04

CARE — Style or Overnight Mask

Blow-dry with thermal defense up to 450°F, air-dry for natural velvet texture, or leave in overnight with a silk scarf for deep weekly cellular renewal.

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is Rejuv4All Hair Oil? add
Rejuv4All Botanical Restorative Hair Elixir is an ultra-concentrated, cold-pressed treatment engineered to revitalize brittle ends, seal raised cuticles, and nurture scalp health. It pairs active plant lipids with lightweight absorption so hair maintains light, touchable movement.
How should I apply the product? add
Warm 2 to 4 drops between palms. Run fingers evenly through clean, towel-dried lengths before blow drying or smoothing across dry hair to finish styling. You can also massage a full pipette into the scalp as an overnight conditioning mask.
How often should I use it? add
Because our formula contains zero heavy waxes, it can be applied daily to dry ends as thermal defense and flyaway control. For deep scalp nourishment, we recommend using it as a pre-wash mask 1 to 2 times weekly.
Is it suitable for my hair type? add
Yes. Rejuv4All is calibrated with universal lipid affinity. It works seamlessly across straight, fine, wavy, coily (Type 1 through 4C), bleached, keratin-treated, and chemically smoothed textures.
What are the ingredients? add
Our signature formula features 100% cold-pressed Moroccan Argan Oil, Japanese Camellia Tsubaki Seed Oil, Virgin Golden Jojoba Ester, Rosemary Leaf Bio-Ferment, Natural Tocopherol (Vitamin E), and subtle botanical extracts of Bergamot and Cedarwood. Zero parabens, sulfates, cyclic silicones, or phthalates.
